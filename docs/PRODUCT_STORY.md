# Apex Resolve for small support teams

An agency client reports a broken login in chat. The developer says it is fixed. The account manager still has to find the original complaint, identify the affected person and ask who actually retested the fix. Apex Resolve keeps the original message, the people involved, the evidence and the independent decision connected.

A customer can use private messaging or group chat on its own. When work needs tracking, a teammate converts one message into an editable issue, identifies the affected people and picks a resolver. A reviewer can follow the link back to the original conversation, subject to access permissions. Existing chat and ticket products can cover this workflow through integrations. Apex Resolve puts the access and review rules inside the same product.

ProofGuard reviews evidence descriptions after consent. It can ask for stronger proof. A workspace reviewer makes the decision, and the database enforces independence.

**Unvalidated pricing hypothesis.** Test a flat monthly fee per agency workspace. Interview an agency owner and two support leads about how many cases need an independent retest and whether that saves enough time to justify a paid pilot. Measure completed cases and repeat use before setting a price.

**Build provenance.** At the V5 checkpoint the code already included messaging, community chat, issue rooms, evidence, analysis and a resolution queue. V5 added affected-party capture, request-bound independent review, historical approval flags and ProofGuard. The repository had no earlier commits. The team's original event-start baseline still needs owner confirmation.
